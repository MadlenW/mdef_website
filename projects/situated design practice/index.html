Situated Design Practice

I love letters. Especially love letters. I am writing at leat one letter or postcard every week to my beloved grandmother, my best friend or my godchild, Camilla. For this, I am taking the advantage of pursuing my letter writing and created four postcards to the people I was able to meet and listen to during the course „Situated Practices“ to reflect on their stories and my own creative practice.
